About Our Department

Democratic Micronation of Wilsonia is dedicated to break away from traditional governance structures and create a more inclusive and participatory democracy. We believe in the power of community participation and our citizens who matter.

Our History

Back in 2023, the Democratic Micronation of Wilsonia broke apart from the United States and established itself as an independent micronation. Why you may ask? Because we belive in the choise of the people, and we want to make sure everyone is heard and valued.You may say the United States is just like that, but they have conflict, rising obesity, and a lot of other problems. We want to create a better future for our citizens and make sure everyone has a choise in our micronation.

FAQ - Democratic Micronation of Wilsonia
If your questions aren't answered here, please contact us.
How do I access my bank account?
You can access your bank account through our secure online banking portal. Visit the "Search" page and click on the "Search government services..." input to find the link to our banking services. Where can I find ATM machines?
ATM machines are simply not needed in Wilsonia! Instead going through the banking portal, you can use our mobile app to access your account and request cash withdrawals, which can be recieved to your location. How do I apply for voter registration?
No need to apply for voter registration! All citizens of Wilsonia are automatically registered to vote. Just make sure to check your eligibility and voting information on the "Calendar" page before voting day. How do i get alerted for deadly/extreme weather?
We have emergency alert system in place that sends notifications to all citizens in the event of severe weather. If deadly weather is expected, alarms will be triggered, but you will be notified in advance. About our EAS system, and our flag
Our EAS system is designed to keep our citizens safe and informed during emergencies. In the event of a severe weather event, our EAS system will send out alerts to all citizens through various channels, including text messages, social media, and our website. We also have a flag that represents our micronation, which features a red and white background, with the USA flag in the top left corner, along with 5 black stars circling a green star, which represents the people who put together the micronation, and Riggs the dog, who unfortunately passed away in 2025 and whose legacy lives on. The flag symbolizes our commitment to democracy, community, and the legacy of those who helped establish Wilsonia.
